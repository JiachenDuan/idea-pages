FAQ

What access do you need? Is it safe?
We use least-privilege access only: a docs CMS role (read/draft), a shared folder for screenshots, and optionally read-only access to a support tag report. No production credentials. NDA optional.
Who publishes changes?
You can choose: (A) we draft and you click publish, or (B) we publish after your written approval in a weekly review thread.
What do you need from us to start?
A link to your help center, your style/voice guidelines (or we’ll propose one), your top 10 support questions, and where screenshots should live.
How do you decide what to update each week?
We prioritize by impact: top-viewed pages, top ticket drivers, and pages tied to onboarding revenue moments (activation/setup/billing).
What’s your retention policy?
We keep only what’s needed to do the work (docs drafts + screenshots) and follow your requested retention window. We can delete all working files on request.
Not legal advice. We don’t handle customer PII; we focus on product docs and public/help-center content.

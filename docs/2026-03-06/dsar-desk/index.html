FAQ

What access do you need?
Least-privilege only. Typical: read-only access for discovery + narrowly-scoped permissions for deletion tasks, plus access to the tools that store user data (e.g., Postgres, Segment destinations, Intercom/Zendesk, S3). No production credentials stored long-term.
How do you handle security and sensitive data?
We work from a dedicated folder/project, keep an access log, and use a retention window. Optional NDA. We don’t need bank access or broad admin rights.
Do you support our stack?
Best fit when your stack is mostly standard SaaS tools + a primary database. We start with a supported set (e.g., Postgres, Segment, S3, Intercom/Zendesk) and add playbooks over time.
Who approves the customer response?
You do. We draft responses based on completed checklists and evidence, and you approve before anything is sent.
What’s onboarding like?
~60–90 minutes across 1–2 calls. You share your current tools, we set up the workflow, and we produce your initial data map + first playbooks.
What’s your retention policy?
Default is minimal retention for active work. You pick a retention window; we delete artifacts on request and document what was removed.
Not legal advice. You remain the source of truth for your privacy posture; we operate the workflow and documentation.

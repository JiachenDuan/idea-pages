FAQ

What do you need from us to start?
A call recording or bullet notes, the customer’s goals, your rough delivery approach, and any constraints (timeline, stack, team). We’ll send a short intake form.
How do you handle security and access?
Least-privilege access only (a shared folder or email forward). NDA optional. No production credentials.
Are you a law firm / do you provide legal advice?
No. We draft business scope documents and coordinate changes; you (or your counsel) approve any legal terms.
What’s onboarding time?
Typically 20–30 minutes for the intake the first time. After that, we can reuse your template and brand voice.
What’s your retention policy?
By default we retain working docs only as long as needed to deliver, then delete on request. You control what stays in your Drive.
Not legal advice. You remain the source of truth for delivery capability and pricing; we package it into a clear proposal and SOW.

FAQ

How do you access customer data safely?
Least-privilege access only. We can work from CSV exports, a shared inbox, or scoped CRM seats. No bank access is needed, and we can operate under NDA with a defined retention window.
What needs to be in place before onboarding?
You need a list of active and recently lapsed members, a clear plan price/renewal policy, and one internal owner who can approve offer rules and escalations.
What is included vs not included?
Included: renewal reminders, failed-payment follow-up, simple save offers, lapsed-member winback, and weekly reporting. Not included: running your entire customer support inbox, dispatching work, or rebuilding your CRM.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually a few days. We import the member list, confirm the approved messaging and save rules, and set the reporting format before the first live queue runs.
How long do you retain member data?
Only as long as needed for the active renewal workflow and reporting window. We can set a fixed retention period and delete exported files on request.

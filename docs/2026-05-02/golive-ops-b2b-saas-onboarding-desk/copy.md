## Before

- Customers wait days for a clear kickoff and next-step plan
- AEs, founders, and engineers keep chasing the same blockers
- Nobody can quickly answer: what is blocking this go-live?

## After
- Every new customer gets a clean kickoff and mutual action plan
- One live blocker tracker with owners, deadlines, and escalation rules
- Weekly customer-ready status updates without founder babysitting
- Faster time-to-value and cleaner handoff into CS/support

## Offer

## Process
1. 15-minute fit call to confirm onboarding volume, motion, and systems
2. Week 1: collect current checklist, define milestones, and launch kickoff cadence
3. Weeks 1-6: run blocker follow-ups, status updates, and owner accountability
4. Handoff: package open items, next steps, and ownership for CS/support

## FAQ
- **What access do you need?**: Usually a shared project tracker, meeting notes, and the customer-facing status thread or inbox. We work with least-privilege access and do not need production credentials.
- **What needs to be true for this to work?**: You need one named internal owner, one customer-side champion, and a reasonably defined onboarding outcome. If nobody owns the relationship, this turns into chaos no matter who runs the checklist.
- **How long does onboarding take?**: Most engagements run 2-6 weeks depending on the customer and integration complexity. We focus on the operating cadence, not the technical implementation itself.
- **How do you handle customer data and retention?**: We only retain the artifacts needed for active onboarding work, prefer redacted or low-sensitivity materials where possible, and can follow a defined deletion window after handoff.
- **What happens during onboarding?**: We get the kickoff moving fast, keep the milestone plan current, chase blockers, and package progress into clear weekly updates so your team and the customer stay aligned.

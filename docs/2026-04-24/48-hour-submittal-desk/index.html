FAQ

How do you handle access and document security?
We use least-privilege access only, usually a shared folder or emailed source docs. No accounting access, no field-system changes, and we can work under your NDA and retention policy.
What do you need from us to start?
At minimum: approved vendor cut sheets, relevant spec sections, project naming preferences, and one person who can answer trade-specific questions within a business day.
Do you replace our PM or engineer?
No. We handle document assembly, formatting, and tracking. Your team still owns technical approval, means-and-methods, and final submission decisions.
How does onboarding work?
We start with one live package, lock the naming and checklist standard, then reuse that workflow for the next jobs. Most shops can start in a day once docs are shared.
What's your retention policy?
By default we retain active-project files only as long as needed to support revisions and closeout. We can follow your required deletion window or archive rules.

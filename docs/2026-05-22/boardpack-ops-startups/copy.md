## FAQ
- **How do you access our metrics and board materials safely?**: Least-privilege access only: shared folders, exports, or view-only dashboards where possible. No production credentials required. Optional NDA and clear access revocation at any time.
- **What do you need from us before you can start?**: A named owner, your current reporting template if one exists, and access to the few systems that hold the numbers or notes you already use. We work best when the source data is already available somewhere, even if it is messy.
- **How long does onboarding take?**: Usually 60-90 minutes total across one kickoff and one async follow-up. After that, the monthly cycle should feel lightweight.
- **Do you keep our materials forever?**: No. We keep only what is needed for active work and agreed archive history. Retention windows can be set up front, and we can delete materials on request.
- **Are you replacing our CFO, finance lead, or chief of staff?**: No. We run the reporting assembly workflow and first-pass drafting. Final numbers, judgment calls, and investor communication stay with your team.

FAQ

How do you access our systems safely?
We work sandbox-first with least-privilege access: test accounts, staging links, reproducible sample data, and time-boxed credentials when needed. No production changes, and no always-on access.
What do you need before this works well?
A named support or CS owner, a place where escalations land, and an approved test environment or dummy account path for repro. We can start lightweight if you already have a support inbox and ticketing tool.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 2-3 business days to define the intake form, severity rules, supported issue types, and handoff template. After that, we can start triaging live escalations.
How long do you retain screenshots or logs?
Only as long as needed for active triage unless you ask for a longer retention window. Default is short-lived artifact storage with deletion after the issue is handed off or closed.
Are you replacing our engineers or support team?
No. We own the messy middle between vague report and usable ticket. Your team still owns technical diagnosis, fixes, and customer relationship decisions.
NDA-friendly. Least-privilege access. Clear retention window. Not a substitute for incident response or debugging ownership.

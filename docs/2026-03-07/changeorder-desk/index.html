FAQ

What access do you need?
Minimal. A shared inbox (or forwarding), and (optionally) read-only access to your project folder/CRM. No bank access, no production systems. We can work from PDFs/photos.
Do you send pricing to the client?
Only after you approve. You stay in control of pricing and client relationship. We’re the paperwork + follow-through layer.
What do we need to have already?
A basic pricebook or at least a ‘how we price labor/materials’ rule of thumb. If you don’t have one, we’ll start with a simple template and refine after the first week.
How fast is onboarding?
Same week. If you can share one sample contract + your preferred change order format, we can be live in 48 hours.
What’s your retention policy?
We retain only what’s needed to run active jobs. Default retention is 90 days after job closeout; you can request shorter/longer or deletion at any time.
We don’t guarantee client approval; we guarantee speed + clarity + documentation. Not legal advice.

FAQ

How do you handle security and access?
Least-privilege only. We work in staging/sandbox environments whenever possible, do not require production credentials, and can use a shared temporary account with a defined retention window. NDA is fine.
What do you need from us to get started?
Usually: access to the demo/staging environment, a rough idea of the buyer flow you want to show, any existing sample data, and one person who can validate the final walkthrough.
How much time will onboarding take?
For most teams, 30-60 minutes for kickoff plus one review pass. The point is to reduce recurring setup work, not create more meetings.
Do you keep our data or credentials?
Only for the active engagement and only what is needed to run the workflow. We can agree on a deletion window up front and rotate/remove access at handoff.
Who is this not a fit for?
Teams with no repeatable demo motion, highly bespoke enterprise implementations, or products that require major custom engineering for every prospect will need a heavier solution than this starter offer.

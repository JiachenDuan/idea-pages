FAQ

What access do you need?
We prefer read-only exports from QuickBooks/Xero/Stripe Invoicing or a weekly CSV. We do not need bank access. If you want us to send emails from your domain, we can use a shared inbox with least-privilege permissions.
Will this damage client relationships?
We run a polite, consistent cadence and never escalate without your explicit approval. You can set tone rules (e.g., no phone calls, no “collections” language, only friendly reminders until day 30).
What do you need from us to start?
A list of open invoices (or AR aging), the best billing contacts, your invoice terms, and your escalation thresholds. Onboarding is typically 60–90 minutes.
How fast is onboarding?
You can be live within 1–2 business days once we have your export and tone/escalation rules.
How do you handle sensitive data and retention?
Least-privilege access only. We store only what’s necessary to run follow-ups (invoice identifiers, amounts, contact emails, status). Default retention is 30 days after offboarding unless you request sooner deletion.
Not legal advice. You control final client-facing escalation language and any decision to pause work.

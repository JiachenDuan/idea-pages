FAQ

How do you access our data safely?
We start with read-only exports from Shopify and your returns tool. No production credentials or payment access required. Optional NDA, least-privilege access, and a defined retention window.
What prerequisites do we need?
You need a Shopify storefront, a way to export returns/orders data, and one person who can confirm product, CX, or ops context when a pattern is unclear.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 2-3 business days. We map your return reasons, align on the taxonomy, and build the first weekly report from the last 60-90 days of data.
Do you change our policies or talk to customers?
No. We analyze and recommend. Your team keeps control of return policy, customer communication, and any site or packaging changes.
What is your retention policy?
By default we retain only the active analysis workspace and latest reports needed for ongoing service. We can delete source exports on request or follow a fixed retention window.

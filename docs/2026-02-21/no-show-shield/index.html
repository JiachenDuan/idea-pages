FAQ

Do you need access to our EHR / scheduling system?
Usually read-only or staff-level access is enough (or we work via exports + your existing messaging tool). We use least-privilege, never ask for billing credentials, and can work under an NDA if needed.
Is this HIPAA compliant?
We design a HIPAA-aware workflow: minimal PHI in texts, consent-first messaging, and clear ‘stop’ keywords. Final compliance choices depend on your systems and policies; you approve everything before launch.
What are the prerequisites?
You need (1) online booking or a clear reschedule path, (2) a way to text patients (existing tool or Twilio), and (3) a staff owner for a 30-minute weekly check-in for the first month.
How long does onboarding take?
About 60–90 minutes total across two short calls, plus sharing your current reminder messages and a few screenshots of your booking flow.
What’s your data retention policy?
We store only what’s needed to operate the workflow (templates, opt-in wording, and aggregated metrics). Patient-level exports can be deleted on request; we can set a default retention window (e.g., 30 days) for any patient lists we touch.

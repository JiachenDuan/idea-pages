FAQ

How do you access our systems safely?
We operate with least privilege: a shared inbox (or forwarding), a sandbox environment if you have one, and read-only access to whatever artifacts you choose to provide (logs/screenshots). No production credentials. Optional NDA and an access log.
What do you need from us to reproduce issues?
Typically: a staging URL (preferred), a test account, and any relevant logs or feature flags. If staging isn't available, we can still triage impact and craft a fix plan from artifacts — we just won’t claim a verified repro.
How do you handle onboarding?
We set up (or clean up) the intake channel, define a severity rubric and escalation rules, and create templates for acknowledgement, clarification questions, and closure. Total time from your team is usually 60–90 minutes.
What’s the retention policy?
Default is minimal retention: we keep only what’s needed for active triage and the audit log. You can choose a retention window (e.g., 30/90 days) and we’ll delete on request.
Will this reduce churn or increase retention?
Indirectly: fast, professional responses improve trust with security-conscious customers and reduce the chance a real issue lingers. The primary measurable benefit is fewer engineer interruptions + tighter response SLAs.

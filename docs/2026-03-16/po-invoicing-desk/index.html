FAQ

What access do you need? Is it safe?
We start with the minimum: a shared billing inbox (optional), portal user accounts with least privilege, and a Drive folder for required docs. No production credentials. We can operate via customer-provided exports and screenshots if you prefer.
Do you need bank access?
No. We don’t touch bank accounts. If a customer requires a banking form, you provide the approved details and we only submit what you approve.
What do you need from us to start?
A list of invoice/PO customers, the last 60 days of submitted invoices (or AR aging), and 2–3 examples of rejected invoices with reasons. We’ll build playbooks from real failures.
How do escalations work?
We define rules. Routine AP nudges run automatically via approved templates. Anything that touches pricing/terms, executive escalation, or threatening language requires your written approval first.
What’s your retention policy?
Default: keep only what’s required for active work + playbooks; delete artifacts on request. We can operate with a short retention window for sensitive docs.
How do you handle ongoing retention/coverage?
This is a recurring desk: a weekly queue review plus monitoring of pending acceptances and expected payment dates. You’ll get a predictable cadence, not one-off heroics.
We operate the workflow and documentation; you remain the source of truth for pricing/terms and approve escalations. Not legal advice.

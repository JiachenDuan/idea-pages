FAQ

What access do you need? Is this safe?
Least-privilege only: a read-only export of PR titles/tickets (or a shared weekly list) and a folder for drafts. No production credentials. NDA optional. We can work from a CSV if you prefer.
What are the prerequisites?
You need a source of truth for what shipped (GitHub PRs, Jira/Linear tickets, or a weekly bullet list). We’ll give you a 10-minute template if you don’t have one.
How fast is onboarding?
Usually 60–90 minutes once. After that, weekly effort on your side is ~20 minutes to confirm what shipped and any caveats.
How do you keep quality consistent over time?
We maintain a tone guide + glossary (product terms, personas, banned phrases), and we do a monthly retro to refine structure and recurring sections.
What’s the retention policy for internal notes/drafts?
Default: keep only final published artifacts + the minimal notes needed for future weeks. You can set a retention window (e.g., 30/60/90 days) and we’ll delete drafts on request.

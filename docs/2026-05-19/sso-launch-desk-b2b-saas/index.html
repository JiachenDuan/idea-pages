FAQ

How do you handle security and access?
We start with least-privilege access and approved artifacts only. In many cases we can work from screenshots, metadata files, test tenants, and customer-admin calls without broad production access. We can work under NDA and follow a defined retention window.
What do we need in place before this works?
You need a working supported SAML and/or SCIM path, one internal owner who can approve mappings and edge cases, and a customer-side admin who can make IdP changes. We are not a substitute for missing product support.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 1-3 business days to set up the templates, supported-flow checklist, and first customer tracker. After that, active customer setups move on the cadence of your team and the customer's admin responsiveness.
Will you stay involved after go-live?
In the starter offer, we own the launch workflow through handoff, then your team takes over steady-state support. If you want ongoing identity-ops help, that is a separate retainer.
What is your retention policy?
By default we retain only the active setup tracker, latest mapping sheet, and final go-live packet needed for current work. We can delete source artifacts on request or follow a fixed retention window you choose.

FAQ

How do you access our support systems safely?
Least-privilege access only. We can work from exported threads, shared channels, or scoped seats in your support tools. No production credentials are required, and we can operate under NDA with a defined retention window.
What do we need before onboarding?
You need a clear definition of what counts as an escalation, enough ticket volume to justify the workflow, and one internal owner who can receive and route the briefs.
Will you talk directly to our customers?
Not by default. The core offer is summary + handoff. We provide a customer-safe update draft, but your team decides what gets sent and when.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually a few days. We define the brief format, supported tools, access scope, and escalation triggers, then start handling flagged tickets in the next cycle.
How long do you retain ticket content?
Only as long as needed for the active workflow and the agreed reporting window. We can set a fixed retention period and delete raw exported context on request.
You keep control of customer communication and prioritization. We provide the summary workflow, handoff brief, and pattern reporting—not incident management or product decisions.

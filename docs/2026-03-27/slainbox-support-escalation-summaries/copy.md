Support has the Zendesk thread, engineering has the Slack side-channel, CS has the angry customer context, and leadership wants an answer now. Instead of resolving the issue, everyone burns time reconstructing what already happened.

- Customer-facing updates are inconsistent or too technical
- Ownership is fuzzy, so escalations bounce around and age badly

## After

- A clear timeline, blocker, owner, and next action

## FAQ
- **How do you access our support systems safely?**: Least-privilege access only. We can work from exported threads, shared channels, or scoped seats in your support tools. No production credentials are required, and we can operate under NDA with a defined retention window.
- **What do we need before onboarding?**: You need a clear definition of what counts as an escalation, enough ticket volume to justify the workflow, and one internal owner who can receive and route the briefs.
- **Will you talk directly to our customers?**: Not by default. The core offer is summary + handoff. We provide a customer-safe update draft, but your team decides what gets sent and when.
- **How long does onboarding take?**: Usually a few days. We define the brief format, supported tools, access scope, and escalation triggers, then start handling flagged tickets in the next cycle.
- **How long do you retain ticket content?**: Only as long as needed for the active workflow and the agreed reporting window. We can set a fixed retention period and delete raw exported context on request.

FAQ

How do you access our data safely?
Least-privilege only: supported platform access, exports, and approved inbox/reporting access when needed. No payment credentials, no unnecessary customer data, optional NDA, and a defined retention window.
What do we need before onboarding?
A supported subscription stack, basic cancel/skip event visibility, and someone who can approve offers and brand voice. If your data is messy, we can still start with a CSV/export-based pilot.
Will this train customers to ask for discounts?
Not if the playbook is designed correctly. We prioritize non-discount saves first-skip, delay, swap, lower cadence-and use discounts sparingly by approved rule.
What happens to customer data after work is done?
We keep only what is needed for active work and agreed reporting. Retention can be time-boxed, and deletion-on-request is part of the operating policy.

FAQ

How do you handle security and account access?
We can work without marketplace logins for many workflows. If access is needed, we use least-privilege access, shared credentials where possible, and document exactly what we touch.
Do you send legal threats or enforce the policy for us?
No. We run the monitoring, evidence, and workflow layer. Your team stays in control of final enforcement, and legal escalation stays with you or your counsel.
What do you need to get started?
A SKU list, your MAP policy or pricing rules, your approved reseller list if you have one, and the channels/sellers you care about first.
How long is onboarding?
Usually 2-5 business days for the starter plan. The first useful monitoring run typically happens in week 1 once scope is clear.
What is your retention policy?
We retain active logs and evidence only as long as needed for ongoing work unless you ask for a longer archive. You can request export and deletion at any time.

FAQ

How do you access our systems safely?
We start with exports wherever possible. If direct access is needed, use least-privilege read-only access only. No store admin actions, no bank access, and no payout execution.
What do you need before onboarding?
Your affiliate platform export, Shopify order/return data, payout rules, and one owner who can answer edge-case policy questions during setup.
Can you handle disputes about commissions?
We label and document exceptions, but you make the final call on disputed payouts. We are the reconciliation workflow, not the contract decision-maker.
How long do you keep the data?
Only as long as needed for active reconciliation and agreed reporting windows. Retention can be shortened by request, and deletion rules should be explicit during onboarding.
What makes a brand a good fit?
Brands with active affiliate revenue, recurring payout cycles, and enough volume that payout week already feels annoying. If you have only a handful of affiliates, this is probably too much process.
Works best when payout rules are explicit, exports are available, and one owner can approve edge-case decisions quickly. Not a fit if you need someone else to own affiliate strategy or contract disputes.

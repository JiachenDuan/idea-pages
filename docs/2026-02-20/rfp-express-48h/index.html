FAQ

What access do you need?
Usually a shared Drive/Dropbox folder (read-only is fine) and a point of contact for clarifications. No production credentials. NDA optional.
What do you need from us to start? (prerequisites)
Any existing policies, diagrams, SOC2 report (if you have one), support/SLA language, and a product overview. If you have nothing, we can still draft a ‘known/unknown’ response, but it will require more approvals.
How fast is onboarding?
Typically 60–90 minutes of your time in week 1 to point us to existing docs and approve your standard language. After that, each RFP needs minimal SME involvement.
How do you handle security/confidential docs?
Least-privilege access only. We can work from redacted documents. We maintain a simple access log and follow a retention window you choose.
What is your retention policy?
Default is ‘retain only what’s needed for active work’; we can delete all materials after delivery or keep a reusable answer bank if you want ongoing help.

What this fixes

A lot of staffing agencies do not have a placement problem. They have a timesheet collection problem in the 24 to 48 hours before payroll cutoff. Contractors forget to submit, client approvers ignore reminders, formats come in wrong, and internal staff lose half a day figuring out what is actually missing. That delay pushes payroll, delays invoices, and makes a growing agency feel more chaotic than it should.

Before
  • Recruiters and ops leads spend Friday afternoon sending the same timesheet reminders again and again
  • Missing, invalid, or unapproved timesheets are buried across email threads, texts, and spreadsheets
  • Payroll and invoicing teams do not get a clean exception list until the last minute
  • Contractors get frustrated when pay is delayed because nobody owned the follow-through
After
  • Every worker sits in a simple timesheet queue with status, next action, and cutoff risk visible
  • Missing or invalid submissions get chased on a repeatable cadence instead of ad hoc panic
  • Client approvals are routed and tracked before they become payroll blockers
  • Your team gets a concise payroll-ready summary instead of a Friday fire drill

FAQ

How do you access contractor and client data safely?
We work from least-privilege access only, usually a shared inbox, exported roster, or limited seat in your existing system. We do not need bank access, payroll-admin rights, or broad system control, and we can work inside a fixed retention window.
What needs to be in place before starting?
You need a repeatable weekly timesheet flow, one clear payroll cutoff, and one internal owner for edge-case escalations. Messy current reminders are fine. That is the pain this service is built for.
What is included versus not included?
Included: reminder cadence, missing-timesheet chase, invalid-entry cleanup, approval routing, and cutoff reporting. Not included: payroll processing, pay-rate decisions, contractor disputes, or full systems implementation.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually a few days. We review one live cutoff cycle, lock the status taxonomy and scripts, and start with one contractor cohort or one weekly run before expanding.
How long do you retain timesheet data?
Only as long as needed for the active workflow and the agreed reporting window. We can delete working files on request after cutoff and archive only the final status output you want to keep.
You keep control of payroll policy, pay rates, contractor communications standards, and final approvals. We run the weekly collection workflow so payroll week stops eating your team.

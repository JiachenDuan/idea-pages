The offer

Included

How it works
  1. 15-min fit call to confirm product flow, stack, and who owns internal approvals
  2. Week 1 setup: audit current demo flow, define the supported path, and build the sample-data + reset package
  3. Run weekly maintenance: QA the flow, refresh seed data, update the issue log, and test the fallback path
  4. Before important meetings: run the pre-demo checklist and flag any blockers with a clear go/no-go note
  5. Monthly review: tighten the script, expand what works, and document recurring failure modes

FAQ

How do you access our systems safely?
Least-privilege access only, limited to the demo environment and the tools needed for the supported workflow. No production credentials, no customer data, optional NDA, and a clear retention window.
What needs to be true before onboarding?
You need one reasonably stable product flow, one internal owner from product or engineering, and permission to maintain a dedicated demo environment or workspace.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 5-7 business days for the first supported flow. Faster if you already have a demo environment and sample data; slower if the current flow is undocumented or unstable.
What if the product breaks outside your scope?
We will flag it quickly, switch to the agreed fallback path when possible, and hand the blocker to your team with reproduction notes. We are owning demo readiness, not core product engineering.
What is your retention policy?
We keep only the artifacts needed to maintain the demo workflow, and we can work within a defined retention window or delete materials at offboarding.

# Keep Enterprise Deals Moving With a Shared Close Plan

**For:** For B2B SaaS teams selling $10k-$150k ACV deals into mid-market and enterprise accounts

## Hero
- Headline: Stop letting qualified enterprise deals die of drift

## Pain
Your team leaves a promising call feeling good, then the deal slows down because next steps live in scattered notes, buyers miss deadlines, and late security or procurement asks show up with no owner. A shared close plan gives everyone one place to see what happens next and what is blocking the deal.

## Before
- Late-stage deals slip two to six weeks without a clear reason
- AEs and founders keep rewriting recap emails from scratch
- Security, legal, and procurement tasks surface too late
- Forecast calls rely on optimism instead of visible next steps

## After
- Every active deal has a clean mutual action plan with named owners
- Meeting recaps turn into concrete next steps the same day
- Blockers get chased before they become silent stalls
- Leadership gets a clearer signal on which deals are real

## FAQ
- **How do you access our deal information safely?**: We work with least-privilege access only: call notes, CRM records, and shared docs as needed. No production systems. You control what buyer-facing messages get sent.
- **What needs to be true for this to work?**: You need at least one real late-stage deal, a named internal owner, and willingness to use one shared close plan. This works best when the buyer already has active momentum, not a dead pipeline opportunity.
- **How long does onboarding take?**: Usually 1-2 business days. We gather your current deal notes, stakeholders, and next steps, then stand up the first plan and reporting rhythm.
- **How long do you retain our deal materials?**: Only for active work unless you ask otherwise. We can work with a defined retention window and delete materials on request.
- **Will you talk directly to our buyer?**: Only if you want that and the motion supports it. By default, we draft and coordinate behind the scenes while your AE or founder stays customer-facing.

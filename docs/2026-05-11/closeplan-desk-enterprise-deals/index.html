FAQ

How do you access our deal information safely?
We work with least-privilege access only: call notes, CRM records, and shared docs as needed. No production systems. You control what buyer-facing messages get sent.
What needs to be true for this to work?
You need at least one real late-stage deal, a named internal owner, and willingness to use one shared close plan. This works best when the buyer already has active momentum, not a dead pipeline opportunity.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 1-2 business days. We gather your current deal notes, stakeholders, and next steps, then stand up the first plan and reporting rhythm.
How long do you retain our deal materials?
Only for active work unless you ask otherwise. We can work with a defined retention window and delete materials on request.
Will you talk directly to our buyer?
Only if you want that and the motion supports it. By default, we draft and coordinate behind the scenes while your AE or founder stays customer-facing.

# ReferralFlow Desk for Specialty Practices

**For:** For specialty practices with inbound referrals, like PT, ortho, derm, imaging, GI, and medspa groups

## Hero
- Headline: Turn inbound referrals into scheduled appointments before they leak
- Subheadline: We run the referral-to-scheduled workflow: intake, missing-doc chase, patient outreach, and status reporting for busy specialty practices.

## Pain
Referral volume leaks when faxed or emailed referrals sit unworked, missing documents are never chased down, and patients do not get contacted fast enough. The result is lost appointments, idle provider capacity, and frustrated referring offices that stop trusting your process.

## Before
- Referrals pile up across fax, email, and sticky-note workflows
- Nobody can quickly tell which patients were called or what is still missing
- Referring offices keep resending paperwork or asking for updates
- Booked visits depend on whoever at the front desk is least overloaded

## After
- Every referral lands in one tracked queue with a clear status
- Missing docs are chased with a repeatable checklist
- Patients get contacted fast with a simple outreach cadence
- You see twice-weekly leakage and booked-appointment reporting

## Offer
ReferralFlow Desk is a done-for-you referral conversion workflow for one location. We log inbound referrals, chase missing paperwork, follow up with patients, and keep the queue moving until each referral is scheduled, declined, or clearly blocked.

### Included
- Shared referral tracker with status taxonomy and owner fields
- Inbound referral logging from fax, email, or uploaded batches
- Missing-doc follow-up templates for referring offices
- Patient outreach cadence and call/text/email script recommendations
- Twice-weekly status summary with booked, blocked, and leaked referrals
- Simple leakage report with top failure reasons

### Not included
- Clinical triage or medical decision-making
- Insurance authorization, billing, or coding work
- Handling emergencies or after-hours patient care
- Deep EHR customization or multi-location rollout in starter tier

## Process
1. 15-minute fit call to confirm specialty, referral volume, and workflow constraints
2. Week 1 setup: tracker, status categories, outreach rules, and intake path
3. Daily/weekly ops: log referrals, chase missing items, and contact patients
4. Twice-weekly review: booked count, blockers, leakage reasons, and next fixes

## FAQ
- **How do you handle security and patient data?**: We use minimum-necessary access only, work inside your approved systems where possible, and can follow a BAA process when required. We do not need broad production access or clinical records beyond what is needed to move the referral workflow.
- **What needs to be in place before we start?**: You need one clear referral intake path, one internal owner for escalations, and basic outreach rules for contacting patients and referring offices. We can work with fax, email, or exports, but the starter tier works best when the volume is manageable and the workflow is not spread across multiple locations.
- **How long does onboarding take?**: Usually 3 to 5 business days for one location. We set up the tracker, agree on statuses and scripts, and start with a live referral batch so the team sees value quickly.
- **What is your retention policy?**: By default, we retain only what is needed for active work and reporting. We can follow a shorter retention window, deletion request process, and your preferred export/archive rules.

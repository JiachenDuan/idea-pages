What this fixes

Referral volume leaks when faxed or emailed referrals sit unworked, missing documents are never chased down, and patients do not get contacted fast enough. The result is lost appointments, idle provider capacity, and frustrated referring offices that stop trusting your process.

Before

  • Nobody can quickly tell which patients were called or what is still missing
  • Referring offices keep resending paperwork or asking for updates

After

FAQ

How do you handle security and patient data?
We use minimum-necessary access only, work inside your approved systems where possible, and can follow a BAA process when required. We do not need broad production access or clinical records beyond what is needed to move the referral workflow.
What needs to be in place before we start?
You need one clear referral intake path, one internal owner for escalations, and basic outreach rules for contacting patients and referring offices. We can work with fax, email, or exports, but the starter tier works best when the volume is manageable and the workflow is not spread across multiple locations.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 3 to 5 business days for one location. We set up the tracker, agree on statuses and scripts, and start with a live referral batch so the team sees value quickly.
What is your retention policy?
By default, we retain only what is needed for active work and reporting. We can follow a shorter retention window, deletion request process, and your preferred export/archive rules.

FAQ

How do you access our CRM safely?
Least-privilege access only (the minimum role needed). We don’t need production credentials. We keep an access log and can work in a dedicated ‘Ops’ user account.
Will you change or close our deals without approval?
No surprises. We agree on close-out rules up front. Any bulk close-out actions are previewed and approved. We include a change log each week.
What do we need to have in place already?
At minimum: a single source-of-truth CRM, named deal stages, and owners assigned for active deals. If stage definitions are messy, we’ll normalize a lightweight definition doc in onboarding.
How long is onboarding and what does it involve?
60–90 minutes. We set access, confirm required fields, define what counts as ‘stale’, and agree on close-out reasons + reporting format.
What’s your retention policy?
We store only what’s needed to operate (typically: a weekly report and change log). We can work entirely inside your tools, and we’ll delete exports on request.

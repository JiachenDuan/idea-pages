FAQ

What access do you need?
Least-privilege only. Ideally a user with permission to open/manage cases and edit listing content. No bank access. We can work via screen-share if you prefer.
Is my data safe (invoices, COAs, supplier info)?
We use a dedicated, access-controlled folder for artifacts. Default retention is 30 days after resolution (configurable). NDA available.
What do you need from me to start?
A screenshot of the suppression reason, the ASIN(s), and any relevant invoices/COA/spec sheets/images. We’ll send a checklist tailored to the suppression type.
What if Amazon keeps rejecting the case?
We’ll iterate with a documented case log and escalate when possible. We can’t guarantee outcomes, but we guarantee a tight process and fast iteration.
How does onboarding work?
Onboarding is a 30–45 minute intake call + a shared checklist. We set up the evidence folder, confirm permissions, and define the SLA and scope for your cases.
How do you prevent recurring suppressions?
After each resolution, we document what triggered it and provide a prevention checklist (content rules, image specs, invoice requirements, variation hygiene). For teams with frequent changes, we offer an optional monthly monitoring add-on.

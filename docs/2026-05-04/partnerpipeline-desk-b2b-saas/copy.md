## Pain
Most SaaS partner programs do not fail because the idea is bad. They fail because nobody owns the weekly follow-through after the agreement gets signed. Referred deals sit untouched, good partners stop hearing from you, and founders end up doing random partner chasing between customer calls. PartnerPipeline Desk turns that vague channel effort into a simple weekly operating system.

## Before
- Your partner list lives in a spreadsheet, a CRM field, and someone's memory
- Signed partners only hear from you when a deal is already slipping
- Referral opportunities go stale because nobody owns follow-up, next steps, or co-sell prep
- You cannot tell which partners are active, trainable, or quietly dead

## After
- Every partner sits in a clear tier with next step, cadence, and owner
- New referrals get chased quickly instead of dying in inboxes
- Important partner calls get a short co-sell brief so your team shows up prepared
- You get a weekly partner health report showing active partners, stuck deals, and what to fix

## Process
1. Fit call: confirm partner type, partner count, current referral flow, and who approves messaging or partner offers
2. Setup: clean the partner roster, define tiers and health rules, lock the weekly cadence, and create the scorecard
3. Run the desk: follow up on partner leads, log partner touches, prep co-sell notes, and escalate anything hot fast
4. Weekly review: you get a clear partner health snapshot, sourced pipeline updates, stale partner risks, and next actions
5. Refine: tighten partner segments, improve enablement prompts, and double down on the partners that actually move deals

## FAQ
- **How do you access partner and deal data safely?**: We use least-privilege access only: usually a CRM seat, shared inbox, partner spreadsheet, or exported reports. No contract-signing authority, no billing access, and no messages sent outside your approved rules.
- **What needs to be in place before starting?**: You need an existing list of partners or referred opportunities, one person who can approve partner messaging, and a basic definition of what counts as an active partner. Messy data is fine; we can start with a spreadsheet.
- **What is included versus excluded?**: Included: partner roster cleanup, weekly touch cadence, referral follow-up, co-sell prep, and weekly reporting. Excluded: quota-carrying sales, negotiating partner contracts, building a PRM, or recruiting entirely net-new partners by default.
- **How long does onboarding take?**: Usually 2-5 business days. We review the current partner list, define the cadence and escalation rules, then start with either stale partners or the next live batch of referred opportunities.
- **How long do you retain partner and opportunity data?**: Only as long as needed for active follow-through and the agreed reporting window. We can work inside a fixed retention period and export final notes back into your system before deleting working files.

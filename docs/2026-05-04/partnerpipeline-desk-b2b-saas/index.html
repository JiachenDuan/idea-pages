What this fixes

Most SaaS partner programs do not fail because the idea is bad. They fail because nobody owns the weekly follow-through after the agreement gets signed. Referred deals sit untouched, good partners stop hearing from you, and founders end up doing random partner chasing between customer calls. PartnerPipeline Desk turns that vague channel effort into a simple weekly operating system.

FAQ

How do you access partner and deal data safely?
We use least-privilege access only: usually a CRM seat, shared inbox, partner spreadsheet, or exported reports. No contract-signing authority, no billing access, and no messages sent outside your approved rules.
What needs to be in place before starting?
You need an existing list of partners or referred opportunities, one person who can approve partner messaging, and a basic definition of what counts as an active partner. Messy data is fine; we can start with a spreadsheet.
What is included versus excluded?
Included: partner roster cleanup, weekly touch cadence, referral follow-up, co-sell prep, and weekly reporting. Excluded: quota-carrying sales, negotiating partner contracts, building a PRM, or recruiting entirely net-new partners by default.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 2-5 business days. We review the current partner list, define the cadence and escalation rules, then start with either stale partners or the next live batch of referred opportunities.
How long do you retain partner and opportunity data?
Only as long as needed for active follow-through and the agreed reporting window. We can work inside a fixed retention period and export final notes back into your system before deleting working files.

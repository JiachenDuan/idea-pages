FAQ

What access do you need?
Best case: read-only CRM access. If that’s not possible, we can work from exports (CSV) + a shared tracker. No production access required. Optional NDA.
Will you email our customers directly?
Only if you want. Default is: we draft messages and you approve/send from your domain. We can also prepare sequences inside your CS tool/CRM for your team to run.
What are the prerequisites to start?
A list of accounts + renewal dates (even if messy), a point owner (CS/RevOps), and 30 minutes to align on your renewal rules (terms, pricing guardrails, stakeholders).
How fast is onboarding?
Most teams are live in 7–14 days. Day 1 is a data pull; by end of week 1 you have a renewal calendar and 90/60/30 sequence.
How do you handle security and retention?
Least-privilege only. We avoid sensitive data; we store only what’s needed to run renewals. You choose a retention window and we delete on request.
Not legal advice. You remain the source of truth for contract terms and customer commitments; we operate the renewal workflow and documentation.

What this fixes

Small contractors do real work all day, then lose their evenings sending scattered photos, rewriting the same progress summary, and answering client texts asking what happened on site. When updates are inconsistent, clients get nervous, owners get pulled into back-and-forth, and later disputes over scope, delays, or payment become harder to untangle. The problem is not construction. It is the communication layer wrapped around it.

Before
  • Crew photos live in text threads, WhatsApp, and someone's camera roll
  • Clients ask for updates because they cannot tell what happened today or what happens next
  • Owners and PMs spend nights writing recap emails instead of selling or managing the next job
  • When a disagreement happens, there is no clean timeline of progress, blockers, and approvals
After
  • Each active job gets one consistent end-of-day update in the same format
  • Clients see progress, blockers, and next steps without chasing your team
  • You build a date-stamped photo log that is useful for payment conversations and disputes
  • The contractor looks organized enough to handle more jobs without adding full-time admin headcount

FAQ

How do you handle access and privacy?
We only need the photos, notes, and recipient list required to produce the updates. No bank access, no estimating software admin rights, and no broader system access unless explicitly scoped. We can follow a fixed retention window for job materials.
What has to be in place before onboarding?
You need one person on your team who can make scope decisions, plus a simple way for crews to send daily inputs, usually text, WhatsApp, email, or a shared folder. You do not need to change your PM system.
What is included versus excluded?
Included: daily update formatting, photo organization, blocker notes, and a running job log. Excluded: scheduling subs, handling client negotiations, dispatch, invoicing, and full project-management responsibilities.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 1 to 3 days. We set the template, confirm recipients, name your active jobs, and test one sample update before going live.
How long do you retain job photos and updates?
Only for the agreed working period and retention window. We can keep a short active-work archive or delete materials after handoff if you prefer.

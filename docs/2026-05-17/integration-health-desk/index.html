FAQ

How do you access our systems safely?
Read-only access only: dashboards, exports, logs, and monitoring views. No production write access. NDA is fine, and we can work inside your existing tools.
What do you need before onboarding?
One named owner, access to the current dashboards or exports, and a list of the integration types you care about most. We start narrow instead of boiling the ocean.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 3-5 business days to map signals, define risk states, and deliver the first useful weekly report.
Do you fix the integrations too?
No. We own monitoring, triage, and coordination. Your team or partner engineers still make product changes, which keeps scope and liability clean.
What is your retention policy?
We keep only the working notes and exports needed for active service. You can set a retention window up front, and we delete on request at offboarding.
We operate the health-review workflow, not your production systems. Least-privilege access, clear owners, and explicit scope boundaries from day one.

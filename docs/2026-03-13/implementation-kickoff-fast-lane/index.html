FAQ

What access do you need?
Usually: the SOW/proposal, sales call notes, and a shared folder. Optional read-only access to your CRM/ticketing. No production credentials. Least-privilege only.
Who owns customer-facing communication?
You do. We draft and coordinate, but you approve anything that goes to the customer.
What prerequisites do you need from us?
A defined target outcome (first value), a rough go-live date, and one internal point of contact (CS/Implementation).
How fast is onboarding / how much time will my team spend?
Most teams spend ~30–60 minutes on intake + 30 minutes on kickoff. After that, we keep momentum via a simple action-item log and weekly status draft.
What’s your retention policy for customer docs?
Default is minimal retention for active work only. You choose a retention window, and we’ll delete on request.

What this fixes

Most contractors do the hard part already: pay for the lead, drive out, inspect the job, and build the estimate. Then the estimate sits. The owner gets busy on jobs, the estimator assumes someone else will follow up, and the customer hires the company that simply stayed in touch. QuoteClose Desk fixes that leak with a clean, polite follow-up system that keeps warm quotes moving.

Before
  • Quotes go out, then sit in a CRM, inbox, or spreadsheet with no clear next step
  • Owners send awkward one-off follow-ups at night after finishing jobsite work
  • Nobody tracks whether the customer is stalled on price, financing, timing, or a competitor bid
  • You cannot see which estimators, lead sources, or quote types actually convert best
After
  • Every new estimate enters a clear 7-21 day follow-up cadence with next step and owner
  • Customers get fast, polite reminders before they mentally move on
  • Price objections, financing concerns, and timing blockers get logged instead of guessed
  • You get a weekly won/lost report that shows where revenue is leaking and what to fix

FAQ

How do you access our customer and quote data safely?
We use least-privilege access only: usually a CRM seat, shared inbox, texting tool, or CSV export. No accounting access, no bank access, and no promises to customers outside your approved scripts.
What has to be in place before starting?
You need a clear quote list, one person who can approve pricing or exceptions, and a basic rule for when a lead is still worth chasing. Your CRM can be messy; a spreadsheet is fine to start.
What is included versus excluded?
Included: follow-up cadence, objection logging, hot-lead surfacing, and weekly reporting. Excluded: custom sales negotiation, site visits, rescoping, lead generation, or replacing your estimator.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 2-5 days. We review one recent batch of quotes, finalize the scripts and escalation rules, then start with either stale quotes or the next live quote batch.
How long do you retain lead and quote data?
Only as long as needed for active follow-up and the agreed reporting window. We can work inside a fixed retention period and export final notes back to your system before deleting working files.

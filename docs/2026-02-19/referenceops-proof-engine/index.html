FAQ

How do you avoid annoying our customers?
Opt-in only, clear caps (e.g., no more than 1 request/quarter per customer), and rotation by segment. We also offer ‘async reference’ options (written answers) when live calls aren’t needed.
What access do you need?
Best case: a shared Drive folder and read-only access to your CRM (or exports). No production credentials. We can work via a dedicated email alias if you prefer.
What are the prerequisites?
At least ~20 active customers (or 10 strong champions), and someone internal who can approve outreach + assets (usually Head of Sales/Marketing).
How fast can we start and what’s onboarding like?
Onboarding is 60–90 minutes total: we align on segments, pull a customer list, and confirm guardrails. Then we run the opt-in outreach and schedule interviews.
What’s your retention policy for customer info?
We store only what’s needed to operate the roster (name, company, segment tags, opt-in status). On request, we’ll delete everything after a defined retention window.

FAQ

What access do you need?
Typically: city portal login (or we submit under your account), a shared inbox for permit comms, and a folder with plans/docs. No bank access. No production systems. Least-privilege only.
Can you work in my city?
Yes if we have (or can build) a repeatable checklist + portal flow. We start with one city + one trade, then expand once the SOP is stable.
How long does onboarding take?
30–45 minutes for the first city/trade setup. After that, each new job intake is ~10 minutes if docs are ready.
What’s your retention policy for plans and customer info?
Default: we retain only what’s needed for active jobs + 30 days after closeout, then delete on request. Longer retention is optional if you want a searchable archive.
What if the city is slow or changes requirements?
We can’t control the AHJ’s queue, but we can keep packages complete, respond quickly to RFIs, and prevent preventable delays with checklists and consistent follow-up.

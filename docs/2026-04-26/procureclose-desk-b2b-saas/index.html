What this fixes

A lot of B2B SaaS deals are effectively won, then sit in procurement because nobody cleanly owns the ugly middle: vendor forms, PO setup, insurance requests, buyer portal updates, missing signatures, and repeated follow-up. That delay hurts forecast accuracy, slows cash collection, and forces AEs or founders to context-switch into project management right when they should be closing the next deal.

FAQ

How do you handle access and sensitive documents safely?
We use least-privilege access only, usually a shared folder, vendor portal seat, exported docs, or email alias. We do not need production credentials, and we work inside an agreed retention window.
What needs to be in place before starting?
You need at least one active deal already in post-yes procurement, one named internal owner, and a way to share standard company artifacts. If your current process is messy, that is normal. This service is built for that stage.
What is included versus not included?
Included: tracking, follow-up drafting, artifact coordination, blocker reporting, and workflow cleanup. Not included: contract negotiation, legal advice, custom buyer-side consulting, or acting as your seller in commercial conversations.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually a few days. We review one active deal, gather your standard documents, lock the tracker format, and start with the highest-likelihood blockers first.
How long do you retain procurement materials?
Only as long as needed for the active closeout workflow and the agreed retention policy. We can delete working files on request once the deal is booked or closed out.

FAQ

How do you access recordings safely?
Least-privilege access only. We work from recorded-call exports or shared call-tracking access, not your broader systems. We can work under NDA and follow a defined deletion window.
What do you need before onboarding?
You need recorded inbound calls, enough weekly call volume to score a meaningful sample, and one owner/manager who can act on the weekly coaching brief.
Will this create team drama?
Only if you use it badly. The framing is no-shame coaching: patterns, examples, and scripts-not public blame. We focus on process gaps and repeatable fixes.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually under a week. We confirm access, define the scoring rubric, align on outcomes, and then the first scored batch goes out in the next weekly cycle.
How long do you keep our recordings and notes?
Only as long as needed for the active engagement and benchmark window you approve. We can set a fixed retention period and delete raw files on request.
You keep control of coaching and staffing decisions. We provide the scoring workflow, examples, and weekly brief-not HR decisions or legal advice.

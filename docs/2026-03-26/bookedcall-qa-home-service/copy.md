# BookedCall QA for Home Service Shops

**For:** For home service companies with recorded inbound calls

## Hero
- Headline: Turn more inbound calls into booked jobs
- Subheadline: BookedCall QA reviews your recorded calls each week, scores the missed revenue moments, and sends a simple coaching brief your team can act on fast.

## Pain
You already pay for calls through SEO, ads, and referrals. The leak happens after the phone rings: missed asks for the appointment, weak handling of price objections, sloppy dispatch questions, or no clear next step. Most owners never see the pattern because they don't have time to listen to recordings at scale.

## Before
- You only hear the disaster calls, not the 20 almost-booked ones
- Front-desk coaching is random and based on anecdotes
- Ad spend gets blamed when the real problem is call handling
- Nobody can say why calls didn't convert by rep or objection type

## After
- A weekly scorecard on a fixed batch of calls
- Tagged missed-booking reasons and objection patterns
- 3-5 concrete coaching actions for the next week
- A repeatable benchmark by rep, source, and call outcome

## Offer
A done-for-you weekly call QA desk for one location: we review a fixed batch of inbound recordings, score them against a booking rubric, and send you a coaching brief with the exact leaks to fix next.

### Included
- Review up to 40 inbound calls per week on the starter plan
- Call scoring rubric tuned for booking quality, not just politeness
- Missed-booking reason tags (price objection, no ask, bad fit triage, slow follow-up, etc.)
- Weekly owner brief with 3-5 coaching priorities
- 2-3 rewritten talk tracks or call snippets for training
- 30-day rolling benchmark so you can see if booking quality improves

### Not included
- Live call answering or receptionist services
- Outbound reactivation campaigns
- Full call center training programs or in-person coaching
- Support for shops without recording access or enough inbound volume

## Process
1. Fit call: confirm niche, call volume, and recording access
2. Week 1 setup: finalize rubric, outcomes taxonomy, and report format
3. Weekly review: score the agreed batch of calls and tag missed-booking reasons
4. Delivery: owner coaching brief + rep-level examples + next-step talk tracks
5. Iteration: refine rubric as patterns emerge and compare week-over-week results

## FAQ
- **How do you access recordings safely?**: Least-privilege access only. We work from recorded-call exports or shared call-tracking access, not your broader systems. We can work under NDA and follow a defined deletion window.
- **What do you need before onboarding?**: You need recorded inbound calls, enough weekly call volume to score a meaningful sample, and one owner/manager who can act on the weekly coaching brief.
- **Will this create team drama?**: Only if you use it badly. The framing is no-shame coaching: patterns, examples, and scripts-not public blame. We focus on process gaps and repeatable fixes.
- **How long does onboarding take?**: Usually under a week. We confirm access, define the scoring rubric, align on outcomes, and then the first scored batch goes out in the next weekly cycle.
- **How long do you keep our recordings and notes?**: Only as long as needed for the active engagement and benchmark window you approve. We can set a fixed retention period and delete raw files on request.

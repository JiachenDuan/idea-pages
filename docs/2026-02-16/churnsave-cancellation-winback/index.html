FAQ

What access do you need?
Read-only access to analytics (e.g., GA/Amplitude/Mixpanel) and billing events (Stripe exports/API as approved). For email, we can draft copy for your team to send, or use a least-privilege role in your email tool. No production credentials.
What prerequisites do we need?
At least ~30 cancellations/month (or meaningful downgrade volume) and a cancellation entry point we can edit (web app or billing portal).
How do we avoid ‘discount addiction’?
We use strict rules: only certain segments get discounts, we prefer pause/downgrade first, and we track whether discounts retain long-term.
How fast is onboarding?
Usually 60–90 minutes total across the first week: a kickoff call plus granting access. We do the build and come back with review-ready copy and configuration.
What’s your retention policy?
By default we retain only the minimum needed for active work and purge within 30 days after the sprint unless you request otherwise.
Can you work with our stack?
Yes for most modern stacks. We start with exports if direct integrations are messy. If your cancellation flow is fully locked inside a third-party portal, we’ll propose a workaround or tell you it’s not a fit.

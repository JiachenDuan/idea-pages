# Customer Slack Desk for B2B SaaS

**For:** For B2B SaaS, AI, infra, and developer-tool teams running shared customer Slack or Teams channels

## Hero
- Headline: Run customer Slack channels without founder ping-pong
- Subheadline: We triage shared customer channels, route issues to the right owner, maintain follow-up SLAs, and send a weekly risk + expansion summary.

## Pain
Shared customer Slack or Teams channels start as a premium experience, then quietly turn into an unowned queue of bugs, onboarding asks, random questions, and churn signals. Engineers get dragged into reactive support, follow-ups slip, and nobody has a clean view of what is urgent, blocked, or growing into renewal risk.

## Before
- Customer messages sit in Slack until the right person happens to notice
- Founders and engineers get pulled into ad hoc support all day
- Nobody knows which threads are blocked, urgent, or renewal-sensitive
- Important asks get answered inconsistently or lost in the scrollback

## After
- Every thread is triaged, tagged, and assigned to the right owner
- Customers get faster, cleaner follow-up without overpromising
- Leadership gets a weekly blocker, churn-risk, and expansion-signal summary
- Shared channels feel high-touch without consuming senior people all day

## Process
1. 15-minute fit call to confirm channel volume, ICP, SLA expectations, and internal owners
2. Set up channel rules, escalation paths, tag taxonomy, and weekly summary format
3. Run daily triage: tag threads, route requests, chase follow-ups, and keep the queue clean
4. Send a weekly summary with open blockers, churn risks, repeated asks, and expansion signals
5. Refine the playbook as patterns emerge so more of the work can be delegated or automated

## FAQ
- **How do you access our customer channels safely?**: Least-privilege access only. We join only the channels you assign, follow your internal escalation rules, and keep exported notes limited to the weekly summary and approved working docs.
- **What needs to be true before onboarding works?**: You need named internal owners for support, product/engineering, and commercial escalations, plus a clear definition of what we can answer directly versus what must be routed.
- **Do you respond directly to customers?**: Yes, if you want that model. We can either triage silently behind the scenes or operate as the first response layer using approved tone, scope, and escalation rules.
- **How long do you retain customer-channel data?**: Only as long as needed for active work and agreed reporting. We recommend a short retention window, store summaries rather than full exports whenever possible, and delete working artifacts on request.
- **Is this a fit if we need full support coverage?**: Usually no. This works best when you need ownership, triage, routing, and visibility in shared channels—not a 24/7 outsourced support team or deep technical implementation bench.

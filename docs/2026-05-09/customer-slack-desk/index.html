FAQ

How do you access our customer channels safely?
Least-privilege access only. We join only the channels you assign, follow your internal escalation rules, and keep exported notes limited to the weekly summary and approved working docs.
What needs to be true before onboarding works?
You need named internal owners for support, product/engineering, and commercial escalations, plus a clear definition of what we can answer directly versus what must be routed.
Do you respond directly to customers?
Yes, if you want that model. We can either triage silently behind the scenes or operate as the first response layer using approved tone, scope, and escalation rules.
How long do you retain customer-channel data?
Only as long as needed for active work and agreed reporting. We recommend a short retention window, store summaries rather than full exports whenever possible, and delete working artifacts on request.
Is this a fit if we need full support coverage?
Usually no. This works best when you need ownership, triage, routing, and visibility in shared channels—not a 24/7 outsourced support team or deep technical implementation bench.

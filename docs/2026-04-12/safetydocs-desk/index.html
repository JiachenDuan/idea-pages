What this fixes

Small subs keep winning work, then get stuck before kickoff because every GC wants a different packet: COI, W-9, OSHA logs, JHA, EMR letter, vendor setup forms, safety acknowledgements, portal uploads, and follow-up emails. FAQ

How do you handle access and sensitive documents?
Use least-privilege shared folders, portal logins only when needed, and keep a simple access log. No banking changes, no hidden submissions, and optional NDA from day one.
What do you need from us to get started?
Your standard company docs, the usual GC packet examples, and one person who can approve missing items fast. The cleaner the base packet, the faster every future job gets.
Do you replace a safety consultant?
No. This is paperwork operations: gathering, packaging, submitting, and chasing status. You remain responsible for the actual safety program and technical compliance.
What is your retention policy?
Keep only what is needed for active packet work and the current playbook. Archive windows can be agreed upfront, and files can be deleted on request after closeout.

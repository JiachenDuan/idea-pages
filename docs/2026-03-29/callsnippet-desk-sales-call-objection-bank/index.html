FAQ

How do you access recordings safely?
Least-privilege access only. We can work from exported recordings, shared folders, or scoped seats in your recording tool. No production access is needed, and we can work under NDA with a defined retention window.
What needs to be in place before onboarding?
You need recorded calls, enough weekly volume to spot patterns, and one internal owner who can receive the brief and help the team apply it.
Do you only cover sales calls?
The starter offer is focused on sales and demo calls. If customer-success or onboarding calls matter too, that can be added in a higher tier once the taxonomy is stable.
How much time does onboarding take?
Usually a few days. We agree on the tagging rubric, what counts as a useful snippet, where recordings live, and how long raw artifacts should be retained.
How long do you retain call content?
Only as long as needed for the active workflow and reporting window. We can set a fixed retention period and delete raw call files on request.

FAQ

How do you handle security and access?
We use least-privilege access only: shared docs, a tracker, and whatever limited internal view is needed to understand setup blockers. No production credentials, and no direct changes inside customer environments unless you explicitly scope that separately.
What needs to be in place before we start?
You need one reasonably repeatable trial motion, a defined ICP, and someone on your team who can answer edge-case product questions. If every trial is fully custom, this is probably too early.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually 2 to 5 business days. Enough to define the first-value milestone, clean up the checklist, and set up the tracker before we touch live trial accounts.
What is your retention policy?
We keep only the documents and notes required for active work, then delete or hand them back on your preferred schedule. Sensitive customer artifacts do not need to live in our systems longer than necessary.

What this fixes

Most home service shops do not have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem after the estimate goes out. The owner gets busy, the office manager is juggling dispatch, and dozens of open quotes sit in limbo with no consistent next step. That means wasted ad spend, slower close rates, and revenue leaking from jobs that were already half sold.

  • Nobody tracks whether the real objection was price, timing, financing, or a competitor bid
  • Owners keep saying they need more leads when the real leak is after the quote
After
  • Every open quote sits in a simple recovery queue with age, status, and next action

FAQ

How do you handle access and customer messaging safely?
We work from least-privilege access only, such as a CRM seat, shared inbox, or exported quote list. All outbound scripts are approved by you first, and we stay inside your stated pricing and scheduling boundaries.
What needs to be in place before starting?
You need a steady stream of estimates, a way to export or view open quotes, and one person on your side who can take hot lead callbacks or approve edge cases. Messy current follow-up is fine. That is the problem this service is designed to fix.
What is included vs not included?
Included: recovery queue setup, follow-up cadence, objection tracking, and weekly handoff/reporting. Not included: rewriting your sales process, changing estimate pricing, dispatching jobs, or promising anything outside your approved script.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually a few days. We review your current quote flow, import the open estimate list, approve scripts, and begin with the quotes most likely to go cold first.
How long do you retain quote and customer data?
Only as long as needed for active follow-up and the agreed reporting window. We can work under a fixed retention policy and delete exported files on request.
You keep authority on pricing, commitments, and customer relationship. We run the boring follow-up discipline so fewer estimates die in silence.

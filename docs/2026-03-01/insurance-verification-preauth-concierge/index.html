FAQ

What access do you need (and how do you keep it safe)?
We use least-privilege access only (read-only schedule export + a secure inbox/folder). No bank access. Optional BAA depending on your setup. We keep an access log and can operate with a retention window (e.g., 30 days) and delete on request.
What do you need from us to start?
A schedule export (or read-only schedule view), your top procedures that require pre-auth, your preferred patient messaging tone, and an escalation contact for edge cases.
How fast is onboarding?
Typically 1–2 business days once access is set. The initial setup call is 60–90 minutes; then we run a small “shadow week” to calibrate.
Will you contact patients directly?
Optional. By default we only provide the issue list + recommended scripts. If you want us to send texts/emails, we do it only from your approved templates and with your approval rules.
How do you handle retention and PHI?
We minimize stored PHI. Operational artifacts (issue list + auth tracker) can be kept in your systems. If we must store anything, we use a defined retention window and delete on request.

What this fixes

Most home service teams do not have a workmanship problem — they have a closeout proof problem. Techs and crews finish the job, but the office still has to chase missing before/after photos, unreadable serial numbers, unsigned checklist items, permit stickers, and weak completion notes before it can invoice confidently or defend a callback.

Before
  • Jobs get marked done even though required photos or checklist items are missing
  • Office staff spends evenings chasing techs for serial numbers, permit photos, and signed proof
  • Invoices go out with weak documentation, which makes disputes and warranty claims harder
  • Owners only notice the pattern after callbacks, delayed collections, or bad reviews
After
  • Every completed job packet is reviewed against a clear proof rubric
  • Missing photos and checklist gaps are flagged the same day
  • The office gets a clean invoice-safe / missing-items status before closing the job
  • Weekly patterns show which technicians, trades, or job types create the most proof leakage

FAQ

How do you access our job photos and closeout data safely?
Least-privilege access only. We can work from a shared folder, exported job packets, or a scoped seat in your field-service system. No bank access is needed, and we can operate under NDA with a defined retention window.
What needs to be in place before onboarding?
You need a clear definition of what proof is required before invoicing or marking a job done, plus a consistent place where job packets land. A simple checklist and shared folder is enough for a pilot.
What is included vs not included?
Included: photo/checklist QA, missing-proof flagging, pass/fix status, and weekly pattern reporting. Not included: dispatch, customer service, rewriting your full CRM, or guaranteeing technical workmanship from photos alone.
How long does onboarding take?
Usually a few days. We define the rubric, confirm the required proof by job type, set the access boundary, and then start reviewing a live batch in the next cycle.
How long do you retain photos and closeout files?
Only as long as needed for the active workflow and agreed reporting window. We can set a fixed retention period and delete exported files on request.
